# Asset sources

See `asset-sources.json` for exact sources and filenames. Original brand assets are reused within the user-authorized Grid & Logic redesign. Stock photography is illustrative, never claimed as actual staff/facilities or catalog merchandise. Unsplash terms: https://unsplash.com/license and https://unsplash.com/terms.

Original garment SVGs are project-created demo catalog illustrations. Fonts are distributed through Google Fonts under their respective SIL Open Font Licenses; font URLs and licensing metadata are recorded in `font-sources.json`. Icons: Lucide (ISC license).

The app favicon (src/app/icon.svg) is an original simple brand glyph made for this implementation. Research website designs were observed for navigation and composition; their layouts and retail/clinic identities were not copied.
