# FORME art direction

Concept: a wardrobe, considered. Fictional fashion label for Pakistan, with adult Eastern and Western collections. Palette: warm ivory #f8f5ef, oxblood #5c222d, graphite #26231f, taupe #a59c8f.
Typography: Cormorant Garamond editorial display and Manrope interface, local WOFF2. Display 44–94px, body 15–18px.
Grid: generous 48px desktop margins, compact utility bar, centered wordmark, separate department strip. Listing 4 columns desktop, 3 tablet, 2 mobile. Spacing 6/12/20/32/48/80/112px.
Hero: split campaign photography and ivory text panel, italic serif headline, small collection index.
Navigation: centered masthead, utility icons, keyboard accessible department disclosure for category links.
Imagery: coherent muted editorial stock fashion photography; product-specific original garment illustrations in a consistent studio catalog treatment, labeled as demo illustrations.
Motion: subtle image scale and cart status feedback; no page scroll effects during shopping.
Distinctive elements: editorial split campaign, open product grid with slim typography, burgundy collection journal panel.
Tokens: ivory/oxblood/taupe → surface/text/active → button/input and product frame styles.
Buttons: rectangular, strong dark fill with compact sans label. Controls: bordered variant selectors, accessible hearts with toggle state, native sorting/filter inputs.
